- Title: Validating the Vitals assessment: A replication study on cognitive assessments and commercial driving risk
- Authors: Alice Elizabeth Atkin; Daniel Scott; Anthony Singhal
- Year: 2024  ·  Journal: Journal of Transport & Health

### Abstract

• Cognitive and sensorimotor task performance can predict on-road driving risk. • We directly replicated the study of commercial drivers reported in Scott et al. (2023. • No differences were observed in predictive value or error rates between each study. • Our results reaffirm use of the Vitals tool in predicting commercial driving risk.
