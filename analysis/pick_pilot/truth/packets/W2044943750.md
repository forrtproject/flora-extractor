### Abstract

I narrowly replicate Holly et al.'s (Journal of Econometrics 2010; 158(1): 160–173) analysis of the housing market in the USA, using the open source R software instead of the original ad hoc GAUSS routines. Their main findings are confirmed and most results are matched exactly. Attention is given to providing a self-contained and fully reproducible analysis, exclusively using user-level features available in the public domain. Copyright © 2014 John Wiley & Sons, Ltd.
